# Hotel Copywriting Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill when creating or editing written content for **Palmiye Nature Hotel & Yacht Club**.

The goal is to write clear, warm, trustworthy hotel copy that fits the real identity of the property and avoids generic AI-generated luxury clichés.

The website should feel:

```txt
boutique
natural
coastal
calm
warm
premium but not exaggerated
direct and useful
```

It should not feel like a generic resort template or an overproduced luxury hotel website.

---

## Project Context

Palmiye Nature Hotel & Yacht Club is a small boutique hotel with:

```txt
14 rooms
restaurant in front of the hotel
marina / yacht club context in front of the hotel
pool area with pool bar behind the hotel
rooftop / terrace bar on the top floor
nature, marina, bay and sunset atmosphere
```

Important room rule:

```txt
The hotel has 14 rooms and all rooms are currently considered the same public room type.
Do not invent room categories such as Deluxe Room, Suite, King Room, Family Room, Sea View Room or Superior Room unless explicitly confirmed later.
```

---

## Positioning

The hotel should be positioned as:

```txt
A peaceful boutique hotel where nature, marina life and sunset views meet.
```

Turkish positioning:

```txt
Doğanın, marinanın ve gün batımı manzaralarının birleştiği sakin bir butik otel deneyimi.
```

The site should sell an experience, not just a bed.

Core experience themes:

```txt
nature
marina
sunset
boutique stay
restaurant
pool bar
rooftop terrace
calm coastal escape
direct reservation contact
```

---

## Tone of Voice

Use a tone that is:

```txt
warm
clear
simple
visual
trustworthy
relaxed
slightly premium
```

Avoid language that is:

```txt
too corporate
too poetic
too generic
too exaggerated
too fake-luxury
too long
```

Good style:

```txt
Wake up close to nature, spend the day by the marina, and end the evening with sunset views from the terrace.
```

Bad style:

```txt
Experience the ultimate paradise of unmatched luxury and world-class exclusive elegance.
```

---

## Content Accuracy Rules

Do not invent hotel features.

Do not claim:

```txt
private beach
direct beachfront
spa
wellness center
all-inclusive
five-star hotel
luxury suites
multiple room types
fine dining
airport transfer
pet-friendly
kids club
gym
conference hall
valet service
Michelin-level restaurant
```

unless explicitly confirmed by the owner later.

Safe claims:

```txt
14-room boutique hotel
restaurant
marina / yacht club atmosphere
pool bar
rooftop / terrace bar
nature
sunset
calm stay
comfortable rooms
direct WhatsApp contact
Marmaris coastal setting
```

When unsure, write conservatively.

## Room Copy Rules

Because all rooms are currently considered the same public room type, write room copy in a general way.

Good:

```txt
Our rooms are designed for a comfortable and calm boutique stay.
```

Good:

```txt
A simple, warm and comfortable room experience for guests who want to stay close to nature and the marina.
```

Bad:

```txt
Choose from Deluxe Sea View Rooms, Family Suites and King Rooms.
```

Bad:

```txt
Our luxury suites offer world-class elegance and exclusive private terraces.
```

If the site needs a room page, call it:

```txt
Rooms
Our Rooms
Standard Room
Boutique Rooms
Comfortable Rooms
```

Do not create fake categories.

## Hero Copy Rules

Hero copy must be short and strong.

Good hero formula:

```txt
[Atmosphere] + [hotel type] + [location/experience]
```

Examples:

```txt
A calm boutique stay by nature, marina and sunset.
```

```txt
Stay close to the marina, surrounded by nature.
```

```txt
A peaceful Marmaris escape with rooms, restaurant, pool bar and rooftop terrace.
```

Turkish examples:

```txt
Doğa, marina ve gün batımı arasında sakin bir butik otel deneyimi.
```

```txt
Marinaya yakın, doğayla iç içe huzurlu bir konaklama.
```

```txt
Marmaris’te oda, restoran, pool bar ve teras barı bir araya getiren butik bir kaçış noktası.
```

Avoid:

```txt
Welcome to paradise
The best hotel in Marmaris
Ultimate luxury experience
Unforgettable moments await
```

## SEO Copy Rules

SEO copy should be natural and specific.

Useful keyword themes:

```txt
Marmaris boutique hotel
Marmaris marina hotel
Marmaris nature hotel
Marmaris hotel with pool bar
Marmaris rooftop terrace bar
Palmiye Nature Hotel
Palmiye Nature Hotel & Yacht Club
```

Do not keyword-stuff.

Good:

```txt
Palmiye Nature Hotel & Yacht Club is a boutique hotel in Marmaris with 14 rooms, a restaurant, pool bar, rooftop terrace and a calm marina-side atmosphere.
```

Bad:

```txt
Marmaris hotel best Marmaris hotel marina Marmaris boutique hotel Marmaris yacht hotel cheap hotel Marmaris.
```

## Bilingual Writing Rules

Initial site languages:

```txt
Turkish
English
```

Future languages:

```txt
German
Russian
```

When writing bilingual content:

- Do not translate word-for-word if it sounds unnatural.
- Preserve facts exactly.
- Keep Turkish warm and natural.
- Keep English clear and international.
- Keep CTA text short in both languages.
- Avoid idioms that do not translate well.

Good Turkish:

```txt
Gününüzü havuz kenarında geçirip akşamı teras barda gün batımıyla tamamlayın.
```

Good English:

```txt
Spend the day by the pool and end the evening with sunset views from the terrace bar.
```

## Content Length Guidelines

For website copy, prefer short blocks.

Recommended lengths:

```txt
Hero title: 6–12 words
Hero subtitle: 15–28 words
Section title: 3–8 words
Section description: 20–45 words
Card title: 2–5 words
Card description: 12–28 words
CTA label: 2–4 words
```

Avoid long paragraphs on the homepage.

Forbidden:

```txt

Slide title

Test text

asdasd
Sample hotel text
```

If final owner-approved copy is missing, write a clean temporary draft that is realistic and fact-safe.

---

## AI Slop Prevention

Before finalizing copy, check:

```txt
Does it sound like a real small boutique hotel?
Is it specific to this property?
Did it invent any features?
Is it too generic?
Is it too luxurious?
Is it short enough for a website?
Can the owner confidently stand behind this sentence?
```

If the answer is no, rewrite.

## Review Checklist

Before finishing a copywriting task, check:

```txt
[ ] Copy matches the real hotel context
[ ] No fake room types were invented
[ ] No unconfirmed hotel features were claimed
[ ] Tone is boutique, natural and calm
[ ] Copy is not generic luxury hotel language
[ ] CTAs are clear and short
[ ] Turkish and English versions preserve the same facts
[ ] SEO copy is natural, not keyword-stuffed
[ ] No placeholder text remains
[ ] Copy can later be moved into content files or admin-managed content
```

---

## Final Principle

Write copy that makes the hotel feel real, attractive and trustworthy.

The best copy for this project is not the most luxurious-sounding copy; it is the clearest, warmest and most accurate expression of the hotel’s actual atmosphere.
